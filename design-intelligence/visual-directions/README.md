# Visual directions

Visual directions describe a coherent visual language across a page or site. They are broader than individual patterns and should guide selection, not act as rigid templates.

Potential vocabulary includes:
- editorial
- minimalist
- luxury
- brutalist
- playful
- technical
- cinematic
- archival
- organic
- experimental

Each direction should eventually document:
- defining visual principles
- typography behavior
- color and surface behavior
- composition and density
- imagery and art direction
- geometry and graphic devices
- motion character
- useful pattern affinities
- common failure modes and unsuitable contexts

Do not equate an industry with a visual direction. A law firm is not automatically minimalist; a technology company is not automatically futuristic. Choose based on the brief, audience, positioning, and content.

No direction is considered a finished preset until it has been documented with examples and counterexamples.
