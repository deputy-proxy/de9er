# Ecommerce Storefront

Use when selling physical or digital products through a catalog or focused product experience.

Priorities: product discovery; accurate photography and variants; transparent pricing, availability, shipping, returns, and taxes; useful product details; trust and support; accessible filtering/sorting; mobile purchase flow.

Do not simulate a functioning cart, checkout, inventory, or payment flow without a real integration. Clearly label prototype-only behavior. Never invent reviews, stock levels, delivery promises, or specifications.
