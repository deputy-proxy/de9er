# Landing Page

Use for a focused page with one primary audience, one offer, and one conversion goal.

## Strategy
1. Identify traffic source and visitor awareness.
2. State audience, problem, offer, and differentiator.
3. Match the first screen to the promise that brought the visitor here.
4. Build a persuasive narrative: attention, relevance, explanation, evidence, objection handling, action.
5. Remove anything that does not advance understanding, trust, or conversion.

## Possible sections
Hero; credibility; problem/context; solution; outcome-led benefits; process; genuine proof; offer/pricing; objections/FAQ; final CTA. Choose and order sections based on the brief. This is not a mandatory checklist.

## Design
Establish one visual thesis. Avoid defaulting to centered text over a gradient. Make the offer understandable even if hero media fails. Use authentic proof only. Keep navigation proportional to the conversion goal. Repeat the CTA at meaningful decision points, not after every paragraph.

## Conversion checks
- Is the audience explicit?
- Is the offer understood within seconds?
- Is the next step obvious and functional?
- Are major claims supported?
- Are price, terms, and expectations clear?
- Does the page work for people who skim?

A button without a real action is not a completed CTA.
