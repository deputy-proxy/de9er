# Website Review

## Visual
- Is there a coherent, distinctive visual thesis?
- Is the hero specific to the audience and offer?
- Is section rhythm intentional?
- Are type, spacing, color, imagery, and geometry consistent?
- Does every section earn its place?

## Content and conversion
- Is the value proposition clear and CTA functional?
- Are claims supported by real evidence?
- Are pricing, limitations, and next steps transparent?
- Remove fabricated testimonials, metrics, logos, and credentials.

## Responsive
Inspect narrow mobile, standard mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop. Check overflow, type scaling, image crops, navigation, and tap targets. No essential content may depend on hover.

## Accessibility
Check landmarks, heading order, keyboard-only use, focus, contrast, form labels/errors, alt text, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Engineering
Check console/network errors, broken links/assets, unnecessary dependencies, image dimensions, lazy loading, and graceful failure of optional libraries.

Report what was actually verified and what was not. Never claim a Lighthouse score or successful form submission without testing it.
